Goldbach conjecture verification, live paper: the statistics of 4×1018 to 5×1018

This page is inspired by, and follows section by section, T. Oliveira e Silva, S. Herzog and S. Pardi, Empirical verification of the even Goldbach conjecture and computation of prime gaps up to 4·1018, Math. Comp. 83 (2014), 2033–2060. Their verification stopped at 4×1018; this project continues it. Each figure below is recomputed in your browser from the verifier's statistics as they arrive, next to their published data up to 4×1018. Numbers refresh every 5 minutes.

Notation as in the paper. p(n) is the smallest prime p such that n − p is prime (the minimal Goldbach partition). S(p) is the least even n with p(n) = p. L(p, x) is the number of even n ≤ x with p(n) = p. ■ gold marks this project's data above 4×1018; grey marks the paper's data.

Table 5, live: first occurrences S(p) found above 4×1018

The paper's table lists 105 primes p ≤ 9769 that are never p(n) for n ≤ 4×1018, so their S(p) is above 4×1018. Because this verification runs contiguously upward from 4×1018, the first even it finds with p(n) = p is S(p) itself: new mathematical data.

2.2 Prime gaps

gk = pk+1 − pk is the gap after the prime pk; P(g) is the smallest prime followed by a gap of exactly g; N(g, x) counts the gaps g with pk+1 ≤ x. The verifier records every gap exactly in count and total length, and every gap ≥ 700 with its first occurrence (a bit-parallel test on every segment); the full N(g) and the sums of gk come from walking every gap of the sampled segments (1 in 256), from the release that added the counter onward.
